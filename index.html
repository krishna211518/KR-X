Privacy & recovery

Uses browser Web Crypto (PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM). The encrypted vault stays in this browser's local storage. If you forget the passphrase or clear this browser's site data, the notes may be permanently unrecoverable. Export the encrypted file and keep it somewhere safe.
